# Video fixture attribution

`highway.mp4` is a short, downscaled excerpt used only as a test fixture for the
video ingest path. It is real highway footage so decoding, keyframe selection,
and captioning are exercised on genuine content.

- **Title:** Motorway A40 - on bridge above the traffic
- **Author:** Kathinka Engels
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Motorway_A40_-_on_bridge_above_the_traffic.webm
- **License:** CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0)
- **Modifications:** trimmed to ~3 seconds, downscaled to 480x270, and
  re-encoded from WebM to MP4. These changes were made by the project.
